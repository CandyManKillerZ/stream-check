When something will not play, one tap tells you whether it is TorBox, a TorBox CDN, AIOStreams, one of its addons, Nuvio’s metadata services — or your own internet.

Good to know
  • Free and open source. Stream Check has no server of its own and collects nothing.
  • Your logins never leave this device except to talk to TorBox and your own AIOStreams server, or to go to your own phone when you pair it over Wi-Fi.
  • Stream Check is an independent tool. It is not made by, or affiliated with, TorBox, AIOStreams or Nuvio.
